SEO that respects both crawlers and AI Overviews.

I'm an SEO Executive currently leading organic growth at Ecomclips, a digital marketing agency, where I run SEO strategy across multiple eCommerce and local business accounts at once. My work spans technical audits, keyword mapping, content-cluster strategy, and Schema markup — plus a growing focus on Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) to keep clients visible inside Google's AI Overviews and other AI-driven answer engines.

I like SEO because it rewards precision: the right keyword, the right internal link, the right piece of structured data can move a business's entire revenue line. I lead client meetings end-to-end — aligning on strategy, reporting results in GA4, and translating rankings into numbers stakeholders actually care about.
